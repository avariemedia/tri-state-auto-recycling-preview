How to find a catalytic converter serial number

The serial is the single most useful thing you can send a buyer. Here's where it usually is and how to get a photo that's actually readable.

Tri-State Auto RecyclingUpdated 2026-10-01

Where to look

  • On the shell — stamped or etched into the body of the converter, often on the flat side.
  • On the heat shield — many OEM units have numbers on the shield rather than the shell.
  • On a tag or plate — some units have a small welded tag.

Numbers are frequently covered by rust, road grime or undercoating. A wire brush lightly over the area usually brings them up. Don't grind — you'll remove the stamp.

How to photograph it

  1. One photo of the whole unit, both pipe ends visible.
  2. One close-up of the number, phone held parallel to the surface.
  3. Use daylight or a flashlight at a low angle — raking light makes stamps readable.
  4. If there are several numbers, shoot all of them.

No number?

Still send the photos. Shape, size, pipe configuration and shield style help identify units, and aftermarket converters often have little or no marking.
