What your shop should never throw away

If it came off a car and it's metal, it probably shouldn't go in the dumpster. Here's the short list, in order of how much it matters.

By Jason Baumlin, owner byline: confirmUpdated 2026-10-01

1. Catalytic converters

The one that matters most. Every removed converter should go in a dedicated tote — not in the scrap bin with rotors, where it ends up sold by weight. Leave the heat shield and any stamped numbers alone. More on converters.

2. Aluminum wheels

Bent or curb-rashed alloys are aluminum, and aluminum is worth more than steel. Keep them separate from rotors.

3. Cores without a credit

Starters, alternators and AC compressors that aren't going back to the parts supplier. One bin. Automotive cores.

4. Rotors and drums

Not individually valuable, but heavy and constant. A scheduled pickup beats paying to haul them out with trash.

5. Radiators and condensers

Aluminum and copper-brass units should be grouped, not mixed with steel.

6. Batteries — but ask first

Never in the trash. Stage upright on a pallet and confirm handling with your recycler before a pickup.

The easiest win in most shops is a second bin. Converters and wheels in one place, everything else in the other.
