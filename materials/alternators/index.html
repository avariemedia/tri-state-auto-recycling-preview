Alternators

Replaced alternators and charging components without a core-return path.

Quick answers

Alternators, explained for shops.

What is it?

Replaced alternators and charging components without a core-return path.

Does my shop need to save it?

Yes, if they are not going back for core credit.

Why does it have value?

Like starters, alternators have copper and aluminum housings that are worth recovering when grouped.

How should we stage it?

Same core bin as starters. Keep them dry.

Does Tri-State collect it?

Yes, with other automotive cores.

How do I add it to a pickup?

Tap “Add to my pickup” above, keep browsing, then build your pickup. Or just text a photo.
