Catalytic Converters

Removed OEM and aftermarket converters from exhaust and emissions repairs, including units still on cut pipe.

Quick answers

Catalytic Converters, explained for shops.

What is it?

Removed OEM and aftermarket converters from exhaust and emissions repairs, including units still on cut pipe.

Does my shop need to save it?

Yes. If your shop does exhaust, emissions or any converter replacements, every removed unit should be set aside.

Why does it have value?

Converters carry recoverable platinum-group metals. Value depends on the specific unit, not its weight — two converters that look alike can be very different.

How should we stage it?

Keep them dry, off the floor and in one tote or shelf — never loose in a mixed scrap bin. Leave serial numbers and shields intact.

Does Tri-State collect it?

Yes — eligible converters are purchased. Each unit is reviewed on its own, and we can look at photos and serial numbers before we arrive.

How do I add it to a pickup?

Tap “Add to my pickup” above, keep browsing, then build your pickup. Or just text a photo.
