Service area · New Jersey

Home turf.

Based at 330 Henry St in South Amboy, Middlesex County. New Jersey is our core service area.

Where we go

Statewide routes run out of South Amboy, with Central Jersey — Middlesex and Monmouth counties — closest to home base.

Who we work with

Dealerships and dealer groups, independent and family-owned repair shops, tire and exhaust shops, fleets and automotive recyclers.

Route roads

NJ Turnpike, Garden State Parkway, Route 9, Route 35, I-287, I-78 and Route 18.

What we collect

We purchase eligible catalytic converters, rims, cores and metal, and can remove other accepted automotive scrap on the same pickup. Full list.
