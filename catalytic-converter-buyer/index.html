Straight answers on converters.

We don't publish a price list. Here's why, and what to expect.

Why no instant price?

Converter value depends on the specific unit and on precious-metal markets that move daily. A number without seeing the unit is a guess. Photos and serials get you a real answer.

Do you buy from individuals?

No. We buy from legitimate commercial automotive businesses. New Jersey law restricts who may purchase detached converters and requires records — see our plain-English summary.

How are converters handled at pickup?

Each one is reviewed on its own and recorded, so you know what left your property.

Is OEM always worth more?

Generally OEM units carry more recoverable material than aftermarket, but there are exceptions. Read the guide.

Cut pipe still attached?

That's fine. Leave shields and stamps intact; don't cut into the shell.
