How a pickup
actually works.

No dumpster to rent. No paperwork marathon. Built around your schedule, not ours.

  1. You reach out

    Call, text a photo, or build a pickup online.

  2. We look first

    Photos and serials let us tell you what's worth separating.

  3. We show up

    Our truck, our crew, at a time that doesn't block your bays.

  4. Itemized

    Converters counted individually. You know what left.

  5. Then it repeats

    On call, or a set day. Your choice.
