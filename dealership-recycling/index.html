What dealership teams ask for

Six things that make a recycling vendor easy to keep.

Clear the service area

Rotors, drums, converters, rims and cores that have built up behind the shop — removed, not rearranged.

Around your hours

Pickups scheduled before the drive opens, after close, or in a window your service manager chooses.

Careful around inventory

We move our truck and material carefully around customer vehicles and lot inventory.

Clean collection

Loaded from your staging area, with the area left clean. Minimal disruption to techs and porters.

Clear valuation & payment

Converters reviewed individually. How material is valued and paid is discussed and agreed before the first pickup.

One accountable contact

The owner is your contact for every repeat pickup — across one store or several rooftops.

Before the first pickup.

Will your crew disrupt the service drive?

No. Pickups happen in a window you choose and are loaded from your staging area.

How are converters recorded?

Individually, at pickup, so your office has a record of what left the property.

How is payment handled?

We agree how material is valued and paid before the first pickup. Converter values move with metal markets, so we don't publish a fixed price list.

Can you cover every rooftop in the group?

Yes, within our service area — one schedule, one contact, coordinated store by store.

Do we need dumpsters or special bins?

No container rental. We'll suggest a simple staging setup using space you already have.

Send it over and we'll complete it.
